📲 How to force iOS/Safari to refresh the icon:
1. Long-press and Delete the previous Aheera Store bookmark from your iPhone home screen.
2. Open Safari and go to http://192.168.1.5:8000/.
3. Tap the Share button (square with arrow) ➔ scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
4. Safari will now use the new green golden crest icon!
